methods.intro.defines-methods-expressions: It says what the chapter covers.

methods.expressions.defines: It points to chapter 5, whose sentences have their own cases.
methods.expressions.says-cast-null: It points to chapters 5 and 8, whose sentences have their own cases.
methods.expressions.array-creation: It points to chapter 6, whose sentences have their own cases.

methods.statements.gives-scope: It points to chapter 1, whose sentences have their own cases.

methods.statements.defines-exceptions-try: It points to chapter 9, whose sentences have their own cases.

methods.foreign.code-outside-specification: It says what the specification leaves out.
methods.foreign.function-writes-outside: It is about the C function, which is outside the language.
